## Starting the offer/ICP stage

You've already given me a named audience (Heads of Risk, mid-market banks), which is a
good starting point but not yet a finished ICP — a title and a vertical aren't the same
as a sub-niche (industry × trigger × company stage) or a scored offer. To build
`ICP-BLUEPRINT.md` properly I need four things, in one batch:

1. **The outcome, not the deliverable.** What does "compliance platform" actually get the
   Head of Risk out of — fewer audit findings, faster regulatory reporting, avoided
   fines, less manual reconciliation? The dream outcome drives everything downstream.
2. **Who's bought before.** What size/type of bank, and what triggered the purchase —
   a new regulation, a failed audit, a leadership change, a specific incident?
3. **Proof** — named results, timeframes, logos you can reference (even anonymized:
   "cut audit prep time 40% for a $3B-AUM regional bank in one quarter").
4. **Current targeting**, if you've run anything before, and what it returned.

Once I have those I'll score the offer against the Value Equation (dream outcome,
perceived likelihood, time delay, effort), name the weakest lever, narrow "mid-market
banks" into an actual sub-niche with a dated trigger, and build the Sales Navigator /
Apollo filter sets (company + contact + exclusions). A rough placeholder to react to:

- **Working sub-niche hypothesis:** mid-market banks ($1B–$50B AUM, adjust as needed)
  that have [a named, dated trigger — e.g., a recent regulatory citation, a new capital
  framework taking effect, or a recent Head-of-Risk hire]. Without a real trigger this
  is just a description, not a targetable segment.
- **Likely weakest lever without proof:** Perceived Likelihood. A stranger's claim about
  a compliance platform carries near-zero weight until it's backed by a specific,
  checkable result — this is usually where B2B offers fail, and compliance/risk is a
  trust-heavy category where it fails hardest.

Send me the four inputs above and I'll turn this into a full `ICP-BLUEPRINT.md` +
`icp.json`, then hand off to list sourcing and copy.
