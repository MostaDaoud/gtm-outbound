# Re: "Build me an outbound campaign" — $29/mo self-serve analytics tool

Before building anything — I don't think outbound is the right instrument here, and I'd
rather say that now than three weeks into domain warmup.

**Running the fit check:**

| Check | Verdict |
|---|---|
| ACV vs. cost to acquire | Fails. $29/month (~$348/year, likely less after churn) against fully-loaded outbound cost — dedicated domains, mailboxes, warmup, a data/enrichment stack, and someone's time to run the sequence — the economics don't close. You'd be underwater on cost-per-booked-call before you ever get to cost-per-customer. |
| Buyer identifiable | Weak. "Individual marketers" is a long tail, not a filterable title/company combination — there's no Sales Navigator or Apollo filter set that cleanly returns this audience without also returning thousands of people who aren't a fit. |
| Problem already known | Plausible, but doesn't rescue the ACV problem on its own. |
| TAM | Huge — which is itself the tell. A market this broad at this price point is a self-serve/volume play, not a 1:1 outreach play. |

**This is the textbook case for product-led growth, not outbound.** Low ACV, self-serve
pricing, individual buyer, huge addressable market — outbound cannot pay for itself at
$29/month. The instrument that fits:

- Free trial or freemium with in-product upgrade prompts
- Content/SEO to capture demand that already exists (marketers searching for this)
- Usage-based triggers that surface high-intent accounts for a *light* human touch
  (not cold outbound — more like a PLG-assisted nudge once someone's already engaged)

**What I'd suggest instead:** if you want, I can help scope the PLG funnel (trial →
activation → upgrade prompts) or a content/SEO plan aimed at this buyer. If there's a
sub-segment of your user base with materially higher willingness-to-pay (agencies buying
seats for a team, for instance — that's a different ACV and a different motion), that
could justify outbound, but that's a distinct campaign built around that segment, not
this $29/month self-serve tier.

I didn't build a list, filter set, or sequence for this, because doing so would produce a
campaign that loses money on every deal it wins — that's the most expensive thing this
process can hand you. Let me know which direction (PLG scoping, or a higher-ACV segment)
you want to go.
