**Prompt:** "Building an outbound campaign for Saudi ecommerce brands doing 1-10M SAR"

Before sizing the list or building sequences, a few things about this market that materially change the plan versus a US/EU ecommerce campaign:

**1. Platform reality — don't default to Shopify**

Saudi ecommerce brands in the 1-10M SAR range are disproportionately on **Salla** and **Zid**, the two dominant Saudi/Gulf-native ecommerce platforms — not Shopify. Both have deep local payment (mada, STC Pay), Arabic-first UX, and Zakat/VAT compliance built in, which is why they've captured the SMB-to-mid-market segment domestically even though Shopify has global mindshare. If your targeting, tech-stack filters, or messaging assume Shopify, you'll under-source the actual market and your personalization will misfire ("I see you're on Shopify" won't land when they're on Salla). Worth checking Salla's and Zid's public app marketplaces / partner directories as a sourcing channel in addition to standard list-building tools.

**2. Contact-data coverage is a real constraint**

Standard data providers (Apollo, ZoomInfo, Clearbit-type tools) have meaningfully thinner, staler coverage in Saudi Arabia and the broader MENA region compared to US/EU/UK — expect noticeably lower match rates on verified emails and mobile numbers, more generic company inboxes, and more outdated title/role data. Practically this means: (a) budget for a higher percentage of manual/LinkedIn-sourced enrichment rather than assuming a clean bulk export, (b) expect your addressable list from any single tool to be a fraction of what the same revenue-band search would return in a Western market, and (c) treat email deliverability testing as more important, not less, since bounce rates on unverified MENA data tend to run higher.

**3. Calendar and cadence**

- The working week in Saudi Arabia is **Sunday–Thursday**, with Friday–Saturday as the weekend. Any send-time optimization, cadence spacing, or "best day to send" assumption carried over from a Monday-Friday market will be off by a day or two — Sunday is effectively "Monday" for open/reply behavior.
- **Ramadan and Eid** materially slow business response — outbound sent during Ramadan (working hours shift, lower engagement) or the Eid holiday windows should either pause or shift to lighter-touch, longer-cycle messaging rather than a standard cadence. Worth checking the Hijri calendar dates for the year you're running the campaign, since Ramadan shifts ~11 days earlier each Gregorian year.

**What I'd do next**

- Build the ICP filter around Salla/Zid merchant signals (public app directories, case studies, partner badges on brand storefronts) rather than generic ecommerce tech-stack tools, supplementing with manual LinkedIn sourcing given the data coverage gap.
- Localize messaging in Arabic (or bilingual) where appropriate for the segment — a 1-10M SAR brand is very plausibly a founder-led, Arabic-first operation.
- Set cadence around the Sun-Thu week and flag the campaign calendar against Ramadan/Eid dates before scheduling sends.
- Plan for a lower list yield per sourcing pass than you'd get in a comparable Western search, and build in extra time for manual enrichment/verification.
