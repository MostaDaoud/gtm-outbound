# Transformational Landing Page: Copy Blueprint Wireframe

Use this fill-in-the-blank blueprint to write a high-converting landing page following Eddie Shleyner's 7-divider architecture.

---

## DIVIDER 1: THE HERO (Clarity & Action)

**[Logo]**: [Brand / Product Logo]

**[Primer Copy]** (Declare category & format; MUST end in colon `:`):  
`[A short, concise [format/deliverable], declared [reputation/standard] for [target audience]: ]`

**[Hero Headline]** (Eugene Schwartz Clean Window; 3–7 words; zero cleverness):  
`[Write Copy, Confidently / Clear Direct Promise]`

**[Hero Subhead]** (Future Pacing; Stop/Start or Imagine):  
`[Stop [current painful state]. Start [experiencing desired transformation and status].]`

**[Hero Body Copy]** (How promise is fulfilled; overcomes main objection):  
`[This [format description], packed with [core mechanism], will teach you how to [achieve tangible payoff without pain point].]`

**[Primary Urgent CTA Button]**:  
`[Buy Now -> Save [Discount%] (Don't delay. Flash sale ends tomorrow.)]`

**[Secondary Downsell CTA]**:  
`[Read Chapter 1 -> Free (Get emailed the first chapter instantly)]`

**[Hero Artwork & Incentive Badge]**:  
`[Visual rendering of product/deliverable + Bright contrasting sticker: "50% OFF FLASH SALE"]`

## DIVIDER 4: THE FASCINATION WALL (Desire & Action)

**[Fascination Headline]**:  
`[[Product Name]: [Scale/Depth framing, e.g. 1 Program. 8 Modules. 100+ Breakthrough Strategies.]]`

**[Fascination Context Subhead]**:  
`Here's a small sample of what you'll discover inside:`

**[The 18-Filter Fascination Grid]** *(Aim for 15–30+ bullets; alternate styles; no work/transactional words)*:
- **Filter #1 (Mechanism)**: The simple "[Mechanism Name]" that instantly [delivers specific payoff]...
- **Filter #2 (Statement+Interest+Benefit)**: [Shocking fact]. But did you know [intriguing twist]? [Concrete benefit].
- **Filter #3 (What)**: What you must do immediately before [event] to [protect/gain outcome]...
- **Filter #4 (What Never)**: What never to do when [common scenario]—and how to [avoid painful mistake].
- **Filter #5 (Right? Wrong)**: [Common practice] is the best way to [goal], right? Wrong! [Truth].
- **Filter #6 (Why)**: Why [unusual phenomenon happens]—and what it means for your [metric/result].
- **Filter #7 (When)**: When [unexpected condition] is actually the best time to [take action].
- **Filter #8 (Specific Question)**: [Burning diagnostic question]? Here's how to [fix it permanently].
- **Filter #9 (If, then)**: If you're currently a [starting status], discovering this one secret will [elevate you to elite status].
- **Filter #10 (Quickest, easiest)**: The quickest, easiest way to [achieve ambition] without [feared sacrifice].
- **Filter #11 (Number)**: [Number] little-known signs that [critical condition]—and what to do about each.
- **Filter #12 (Big Promise)**: [VERB IN ALL CAPS] [MASSIVE BENEFIT] without [frustrating chore]!
- **Filter #13 (How)**: How to [attain desirable outcome] using only [ordinary accessible asset]!
- **Filter #14 (Caution/Warning)**: CAUTION: The common [mistake] everyone thinks is helpful—that actually [destroys result].
- **Filter #15 (Truth)**: THE TRUTH ABOUT [CONTROVERSIAL TOPIC]: What [insiders] won't tell you.
- **Filter #16 (Best)**: BETTER THAN [STANDARD SOLUTION]: The uncanny [alternative] that delivers [superior result].
- **Filter #17 (Surprising)**: Can [simple action] really [solve huge problem]? (The data may surprise you).
- **Filter #18 (Are you)**: Are you making these [number] dangerous mistakes with your [domain]?

**[Urgent CTA Button]**:  
`[Buy Now -> Save [Discount%] (Don't delay. Flash sale ends tomorrow.)]`
