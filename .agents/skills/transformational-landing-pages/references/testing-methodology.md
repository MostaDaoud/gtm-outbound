# Testing Methodology: Big Differences vs. Small Tests

Direct-response legend Eugene Schwartz established the fundamental doctrine of conversion optimization:

> *"Failing often, and testing big differences, shows you are doing enough as a copywriter."*  
> — Eugene Schwartz

---

## 1. Big Tests (The Search for Breakthroughs)

### The Objective
Big tests aim for **2x to 3x conversion multipliers**, not marginal 2% to 5% incremental gains.

### What Constitutes a Big Test?
A big test pits two radically different concepts against each other:
- A completely restructured 7-divider layout vs. a standard short-form control.
- An entirely new emotional angle (e.g., Imposter fear vs. Financial freedom).
- A wall of 25 fascinations vs. a feature bullet list.
- A new Hero promise, subhead, and visual art.

### The Big Test Failure Protocol
If a radical transformation fails to beat the control, do not abandon the framework. Execute the troubleshooting loop:
1. **Revisit the Person (sans 'a')**: Did you speak to the right human?
2. **Re-evaluate the Problem**: Did you tap into their primary crisis or a secondary irritation?
3. **Rewrite the Hero Trio**: Revise the Headline, Subhead, and Body Copy while retaining your fascinations, proof points, and guarantees.
4. **Retest against the control**.

---

## 2. Small Tests (Single-Variable Optimization)

### When to Run Small Tests
Only after a Big Test achieves a transformational breakthrough (e.g., doubling baseline conversions) should you pivot to small tests. The winning contender becomes your new control.

### High-Impact Small Test Variables
1. **The Incentive (Eddie's Top Pick)**: Shift from a 50% discount to an exclusive bonus bundle, free trial, or extended access.
2. **The Hero Artwork**: Test 3D product mockup vs. product-in-use photograph vs. video thumbnail.
3. **The CTA Button Accent Color**: Test vivid high-contrast tones (e.g., safety orange vs. bright lime green).
4. **The Downsell Offer**: Test free chapter PDF vs. 5-minute video training vs. cheat sheet download.
