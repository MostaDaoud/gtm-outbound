# The Fascination Vault: 18 Filters & 9 Guardrails

A **fascination** is a provocative, one-to-three sentence bullet that teases what a reader will discover inside a piece of content without giving the secret away. First perfected by Mel Martin and Eugene Schwartz for Boardroom mail-order promotions, fascinations trigger the human *curiosity gap* (George Loewenstein).

---

## The 9 Guardrails of Fascination Writing

1. **Write more than you need**: Draft 3x to 4x the target number. Cull ruthlessly, keeping only the top 25%.
2. **The more specific the benefit, the better**: Vague promises fail. Specify exact percentages, page numbers, ingredients, or timelines.
3. **Prove it**: Ground claims in external proof markers (`Page 14`, `Columbia University study`, `used by the Chicago Cubs`).
4. **Clarity beats concision**: If adding three words makes the benefit clearer or more vivid, keep them.
5. **Use simple words**: Avoid pretentious vocabulary. Simple words enter the brain without friction.
6. **Use the prospect's vocabulary**: Echo the exact phrasing gathered during audience interviews.
7. **Avoid "transactional" words**: Never use `buy`, `purchase`, or `pay` in fascinations. Use `get`, `claim`, `discover`.
8. **Avoid "work" words**: Never use `learn`, `study`, or `read`. Readers hate effort. Use `discover`, `uncover`, `reveal`.
9. **Create variance**: Break monotonous list reading by varying length, structure, and filter openings.

---

## The 3-Step Creation Process
1. **Find the Nugget**: Scour content for valuable insights paired with a prospect benefit.
2. **Tease the Value & State the Benefit**: Conceal the operational mechanism while making the payoff vivid.
3. **Apply a Filter**: Slot the statement into one of the 18 proven filters below.

---

## The 18 Fascination Filters & Swipe Library

### Filter #1: Mechanisms
- **Concept**: Names a unique or proprietary mechanism to make an old benefit feel fresh and defensible.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"The simple 'pinch test' that instantly tells what your body fat levels are—with pinpoint .5% accuracy."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"The 'optical illusion' that absolutely destroys most golfers... the single most damaging 'habit' nearly all amateurs suffer."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"How to use the 'hip-swinging secret' soldiers use to drop opponents instantly—no speed or strength required!"* (Gary Halbert)

### Filter #2: Statement + Interest + Benefit
- **Concept**: A 3-part narrative arc: (1) Surprising fact, (2) Intriguing wrinkle, (3) Direct payoff.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"Drowning is the third leading cause of accidental death. But did you know it's possible to save a drowning person even if you can't swim? Page 15."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"You walk into an empty self-service elevator late at night and press the button. Before the door closes, you get back out fast. Why? (Nine ways to outwit a mugger.) Page 380."* (Clayton Makepeace)

### Filter #3: “What…”
- **Concept**: Uses the word 'What' to conceal an action or high-value piece of information.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"What you must do immediately before November 15th to preserve your wealth."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"What women really want from a man! (Nine of ten men are absolutely floored by this secret!)"* (Gary Halbert)
  - *"What every catalog mailer in the world does wrong, month after month... and how to create your own 'gold mine' from scratch!"* (Gary Halbert)

### Filter #4: “What never…”
- **Concept**: Leverages loss aversion. People sprint from pain faster than they run to pleasure.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"What never to eat on an airplane. The dirtiest, deadliest airplane in the whole wide world."* (Mel Martin)
  - *"What never to keep in your safety deposit box. Never. And how to stop the state from seizing it."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"What you should NEVER do before surgery. Doing so slows recovery and can even cause amnesia!"* (Kim Krause Schwalm)

### Filter #5: “Right? Wrong…”
- **Concept**: Debunks a deeply held industry belief or common myth, turning the prospect's assumption on its head.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"Sneezing into a tissue prevents colds, right? Wrong! Page 2 explains why."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"The medicine cabinet is the best place to store medicine, right? Wrong! It's the worst. Facts on page 120."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"Drinking wine kills your memory, right? Wrong! Breakthrough Columbia University study reveals drinking this daily actually lowers Alzheimer's risk by almost HALF."* (Kim Krause Schwalm)

### Filter #6: “Why…”
- **Concept**: Taps into fundamental curiosity by answering why an unusual or counterintuitive phenomenon occurs.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"Why your weight, strength, and agility are the least important parts of winning a street altercation!"* (Gary Halbert)
  - *"Why was baldness almost unheard of in Japan before WWII... but rampant among Japanese men today? See page 334."* (Gary Bencivenga)
  - *"Why you can't trust your pension fund: Dirty tricks they play with your money—and how to stop them COLD."* (Clayton Makepeace)

### Filter #7: “When…”
- **Concept**: Reveals critical timing or a conditional rule for maximum success.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"When the IRS has to pay you interest! If they miss this deadline by a single day, they're legally obligated to do so. Page 15."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"When cold pizza is the perfect fat-loss breakfast. Page 14."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"When to use the secret '3-Week Repeat' technique to increase direct mail campaign results by one-third!"* (Gary Halbert)

### Filter #8: Specific Question
- **Concept**: Poses an arresting question that halts the reader, forcing their brain to subconsciously search for an answer.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"Do you know that one-quarter of all household burglars gain entrance without breaking in? Here's how... and how to stop them."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"High cholesterol? 'Actually reverses plaque in your arteries.' See page 349."* (Gary Bencivenga)
  - *"Ingrown toenail? Here's how to get rid of it permanently. Page 383."* (Gary Bencivenga)

### Filter #9: “If, then…”
- **Concept**: Qualifies the reader instantly. If the condition fits, the promise feels custom-built for them.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"If you have a tension headache, here's why you should forget your scalp and concentrate on your toes."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"If you've got just 20 minutes a month, I guarantee to work a financial miracle in your life."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"If you are now a 'B' copywriter, by discovering this one unusual secret, you will quickly move into the rarefied ranks of 'A' writers commanding huge royalties."* (Clayton Makepeace)

### Filter #10: “Quickest, easiest…”
- **Concept**: Appeals to human laziness and the desire for turn-key, low-effort shortcuts.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"The easiest way to beat out even ferocious competition, even when their budget is huge and their copy is brilliant."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"The quickest, easiest way to relieve migraine pain. Just add this to a glass of water and drink it."* (Kim Krause Schwalm)
  - *"The 10 easiest markets rookie entrepreneurs can tap for maximum profit with minimum risk!"* (Gary Halbert)

### Filter #11: Number
- **Concept**: Groups multiple insights into a tangible numerical list, multiplying perceived value.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"The seven little-known signs of internal bleeding and what to do about each—page 4."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"3 often overlooked investment vehicles that can make you up to 10 times richer when the next scandal breaks."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"9 steps to protect your home-based business from the IRS. Plus, one risky pitfall to avoid."* (Kim Krause Schwalm)

### Filter #12: Big Promise
- **Concept**: Leads with an audacious, direct transformation starting with an action verb.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"BUILD ENORMOUS MUSCLE TONE without exercise! Ten-minute breakthrough naturally triples growth hormone."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"BANISH MENSTRUAL CRAMPS FOR GOOD with a common vitamin! Wipes out over 80% of all cases."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"The biggest tax break of your life: New rules that allow you to pay ZERO taxes when you sell your home."* (Kim Krause Schwalm)

### Filter #13: “How…”
- **Concept**: The classic direct-response workhorse. Pairs an ambition with a concrete solution.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"How to rub your stomach away: The simplest and most natural way to lose weight is this effortless 2-minute exercise."* (Eugene Schwartz)
  - *"How to get the IRS to forgive your debt. Older adults meeting this criterion get off the hook for back taxes."* (Kim Krause Schwalm)
  - *"How to gain instant relief from a leg cramp—with apple cider vinegar!"* (Gary Bencivenga)

### Filter #14: “Caution…” / “Warning…”
- **Concept**: Creates an urgent stop signal by exposing hidden hazards or unseen pitfalls.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"WARNING: Your #1 asset is now in extreme danger! Why home equity may be vulnerable to attack."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"BEWARE! The common food everyone thinks is so 'healthy'—yet actually depresses your immune system! Page 215."* (Gary Bencivenga)
  - *"Warning! The popular weight-loss supplement that causes osteoporosis while claiming to be 100% safe!"* (Kim Krause Schwalm)

### Filter #15: “Truth…”
- **Concept**: Leverages controversy and industry insider disclosures.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"THE TRUTH ABOUT MUTUAL FUNDS: What your broker doesn't tell you could cost you up to 5% this year."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"THE TRUTH ABOUT TUNA: Is it good for you, or does it contain harmful metals that ruin your immune system? Page 70."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"The real reason people choose to buy anything—the secret truth long known by master salesmen finally revealed!"* (Gary Halbert)

### Filter #16: “Best…”
- **Concept**: Appeals to the reader's ego and status desire to possess the finest solution available.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"BETTER THAN THE BENCH PRESS: For a broader, more muscular chest, the uncanny exercise on page 17 works wonders."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"Better than Botox: The beauty secret Hollywood actresses use to look 15 years younger than their true age."* (Kim Krause Schwalm)
  - *"Urinary tract infections? The best treatment is also the cheapest. See page 546."* (Gary Bencivenga)

### Filter #17: “Surprising…”
- **Concept**: Promises counterintuitive findings that challenge established consensus.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"Can this fruit lower blood pressure? Research shows this surprisingly powerful extract has dramatic effects!"* (Kim Krause Schwalm)
  - *"Are short subject lines really better than long ones? (The answer may surprise you.)"* (Gary Halbert)

### Filter #18: “Are you…” / “Do you…”
- **Concept**: Personal diagnosis and FOMO. Forces immediate self-evaluation.
- **Swipes**:
  - *"Are you and your doctor making these common mistakes with your health? M.D. exposes 9 deadly flaws."* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"Do you take any of these 16 popular vitamins or supplements? If so, please read this M.D.'s urgent warning!"* (Clayton Makepeace)
  - *"Does your broker have a 'rap sheet' with the SEC? Here's how to find out—FAST!"* (Clayton Makepeace)
