# Copy Lab GCC Specialist (خبير كتابة المحتوى والإعلانات الخليجية)

Dedicated direct-response and scriptwriting engine for the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC), grounded in the **Gulf Content Diploma for Professionals**.

---

## 1. Dialect Selection & Registers

When writing or rewriting, specify or default to the target market:

| Register | Target Use Case | Signature Linguistic Patterns |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Urban Najdi (نجدي حضري)** | KSA digital commerce, youth brands, Reels, apps | `وش`, `ليه`, `بالدوام`, `اللي`, `بنسوي`, `يمديك`, `ذا` |
| **Urban Emirati (إماراتي حضري)** | UAE brand campaigns, luxury, local lifestyle | `شو`, `ليش`, `بالبيت`, `بنسوي`, `هذا`, `وايد` |
| **White Gulf Arabic (لهجة بيضاء)** | Pan-GCC B2B SaaS, corporate tech, consulting | Clean conversational Arabic accessible to all 6 Gulf states |
| **Hijazi (حجازي)** | Western Saudi lifestyle, cafes, creative arts | `إيش`, `كيفك`, `دحين`, `عشان`, `حقنا` |

## 2. Core Workflows

### Workflow 1: Short-Form Video Script (Reels / TikTok / Shorts)
1. **Hook (0-3s)**: Cultural curiosity gap or shared pain point (consult 12 Archetypes in `../copy-lab/references/gcc-copywriting.md`).
2. **Setup (3-15s)**: Relatable daily scenario (traffic, work, coffee ritual, Deerah visit).
3. **Core Insight (15-45s)**: The high-value tip, demo, or counter-intuitive shift.
4. **Call to Action (45-60s)**: Clear, frictionless step (comment keyword, link in bio, WhatsApp).

### Workflow 2: Seasonal Campaign Copy (Foundation Day / National Day / Ramadan)
1. Select appropriate emotional tone from `../copy-lab/references/gcc-copywriting.md`:
   * **Foundation Day (Feb 22)**: Heritage, 300 years of pride, Diriyah roots, traditional dress.
   * **National Day (Sep 23)**: Modern unity, Vision 2030, futuristic ambition, aviation and green pride.
   * **Ramadan**: Generosity, nocturnal schedule, family gatherings, spiritual reflection.
2. Draft hero headlines, subheads, and offer terms with regional urgency.

### Workflow 3: Direct-Response Sales Page / VSL
1. Structure using Copy Lab's core direct-response principles (Schwartz awareness stages, Hormozi offer structure).
2. Localize all proof points, testimonials, currencies (SAR, AED, KWD), and payment gateways (Mada, Apple Pay, Tabby/Tamara).
3. Enforce the **Anti-Slop Contract**: Zero literal translated English idioms.

---

## 3. The GCC Anti-Slop Contract

Before delivering Arabic copy, verify:
* [ ] Zero translated English idioms (*مغير قواعد اللعبة*, *سلس وموثوق*, *دع ذلك يغرق*).
* [ ] Zero Egyptian or Levantine slips (*عشان كدة*, *هتعمل*, *بتاع*, *شو هاد*, *هيك*).
* [ ] Proper standard Arabic spelling for social posts (*ض* vs *ظ* preserved correctly).
* [ ] Realistic, authentic social markers (*أقلط*, *حياك*, *لاهنت*, *كفو*, *سنع*, *يمديك*).
* [ ] No em-dashes (` - `) in copy.
