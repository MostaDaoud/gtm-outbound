# GCC & Gulf Cultural Copywriting Review Lens (`gcc-lens.md`)

Audit and critique Arabic copy, video scripts, ad copy, and campaigns targeted at audiences in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, and Oman.

---

## The 100-Point GCC Scoring Rubric

Evaluate the copy across five 20-point dimensions:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        GCC Copy Audit Scorecard                        │
```

### Category Breakdown

### 1. Dialect Integrity & Consistency (20 pts)
* **Check for dialect clashes**: Does the copy accidentally use Egyptian markers (*عشان كدة*, *هتعمل*, *بتاع*), Levantine markers (*هيك*, *شو هاد*, *منشان*), or unnatural blends?
* **Orthography vs Phonetics**: Are words spelled correctly according to standard Arabic orthography (*رمضان*, *مضبوط*, *حفظ*) rather than corrupted spoken phonetics, while preserving authentic conversational vocabulary (*أقلط*, *يمديك*, *سنع*)?
* **Persona alignment**: If targeting Riyadh youth, does it use natural Urban Najdi? If targeting pan-GCC B2B, is it clean White Gulf Arabic?

### 2. Cultural Resonance & Nuance (20 pts)
* **Contextual accuracy**: Does the copy demonstrate genuine understanding of regional realities (e.g., Deerah return during holidays, afternoon heat, nocturnal Ramadan routines, Riyadh Season buzz)?
* **Respectful hospitality & social etiquette**: Are social codes used correctly (*لاهنت*, *حياك*, *كفو*, *فزعة*) without feeling forced or mocked?
* **Sub-region distinction**: Does it avoid treating Saudi Arabia as a single monoculture (distinguishing Najd, Hijaz, Eastern Province, Southern, and Northern nuances)?

### 3. Anti-AI Arabic Slop & Purity (20 pts)
* **Zero translated idioms**: Are there literal translations of Western marketing cliches (*مغير قواعد اللعبة*, *سلس وموثوق*, *دع ذلك يستقر في ذهنك*)?
* **Natural sentence flow (Burstiness)**: Is the rhythm natural and varied, mixing short punchy statements with descriptive flow, avoiding robotic repetitive sentence openers (*في عالم اليوم المليء بـ...*)?
* **No hollow fluff**: Does the copy deliver concrete facts, specifics, and relatable examples rather than vague claims?

### 4. Hook Strength & Regional Relevance (20 pts)
* **First 3 seconds / headline power**: Does the hook immediately stop the scroll by targeting a specific shared frustration, curiosity gap, or aspirational desire?
* **Relatable imagery**: Does the copy invoke concrete everyday experiences (coffee routines, workplace scenarios, family gatherings, weekend camping)?

### 5. Direct-Response Conversion & CTA (20 pts)
* **Clear economic incentive**: Is the offer specific, believable, and compelling?
* **Frictionless local conversion**: Does the call to action align with regional user habits (e.g., direct WhatsApp booking, local payment methods like Mada/Apple Pay, installment services like Tamara/Tabby)?
* **Risk reversal**: Is there a clear, high-trust guarantee or reassurance that overcomes buyer hesitation?

## Lens Output Format

When running this lens, return:

1. **Overall GCC Score**: [Score / 100] + Tier (Poor < 60, Mediocre 60-79, Strong 80-89, Elite 90-100).
2. **Critical Dialect & Cultural Flaws**: List specific lines with improper dialect, translation artifacts, or cultural disconnects.
3. **The Anti-Slop Hit List**: Any robotic AI phrases or banned non-Gulf idioms detected.
4. **Before vs After Rewrite Table**: Show specific line-by-line transformations improving cultural resonance, dialect consistency, and conversion power.
